Rui found two different reasons an otherwise useful mesh can fail to export. One is floating-point roundoff and is fixed here. The other changes the connection geometry and remains an explicit limitation.

Read Rui's review · Reproduced locally with FloPy 3.11 and MODFLOW-USG 1.5.

01 · Boundary centres Fixed

The centre is outside by less than a trillionth of a metre

The actual concave-domain mesh

Voronoi cellsRoundoff-affected centres

Magnified view normal to a boundary

Gap and band are exaggerated independently for visibility. Switching the rule never moves the centre.

Why it fails: clipping a sloping edge leaves a few generators a handful of floating-point steps outside the polygon. Exact containment rejects them.

Applied fix: accept a centre when its distance from the polygon is at most 32 × machine epsilon × coordinate scale. Preserve the input coordinates. A centre meaningfully outside the cell still raises an error.

02 · Numerical overlap slivers Fixed

A microscopic overlap must not erase a connection

Schematic test fixture; the strip is vastly exaggerated. The regression uses overlaps of two floating-point steps, at both local and projected coordinates.

Applied fix: forgive an overlap only when it lies entirely within the roundoff band of both cell boundaries. Recover the common face using that numerical precision, so the reciprocal connection survives. Preserve the source polygons.

A small area alone is insufficient: a tiny patch can still penetrate well inside both cells. Tests reject that case and confirm that corner-only contacts do not become flow connections.

03 · Kinked barriers Deferred · documented

A bent face has more than one normal direction

The two actual cells at the failing connection

This is real geometry, not roundoff. Raising the coordinate tolerance to accept it would conceal a modelling decision.

For a straight shared face, each centre has one perpendicular distance to it. At a kink, each segment has its own length and normal direction. Joining the endpoints with a straight chord changes the interface.

Two possible future solutions:

  • Change the mesh: split or remesh around the kink so neighbouring cells share straight faces. Recheck centres and orthogonality.
  • Combine segment conductances: retain the total face length and derive an effective distance. Validate heads and flow rates before supporting this approximation.

Select the candidate overlay to see the proposed single numerical connection while retaining the segmented face. No effective-distance rule is implemented here. A simple average of segment distances is not generally equivalent to adding their conductances. Heterogeneous conductivity and nonorthogonality need particular care.

04 · Stronger tests Implemented

The right heads can hide the wrong amount of water

With fixed heads at both ends and uniform conductivity, scaling every conductance leaves the head profile unchanged. Flow scales with conductance. Even a balanced water budget cannot identify that mistake.
